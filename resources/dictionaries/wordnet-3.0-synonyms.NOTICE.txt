Kalam bundled dictionary notice — WordNet synonyms & antonyms
=============================================================

Data name: Kalam English Synonyms 3.0 / Kalam English Antonyms 3.0
Data format: generated TSV (word<TAB>definition), gzip-compressed

These two packs are derived from the Princeton WordNet 3.0 database:

- english-synonyms-3.0.tsv.gz — for every WordNet word, the other words
  that share one of its synsets, rendered as
  `word<TAB>synonyms: a, b, c` (up to 20, deduplicated, underscores in
  multi-word terms replaced with spaces). 110,365 entries.
- english-antonyms-3.0.tsv.gz — word pairs linked by the WordNet `!`
  (antonym) pointer, rendered as `word<TAB>antonyms: a, b`.
  6,621 entries.

Upstream source:
Princeton WordNet 3.0, Princeton University (wordnet.princeton.edu). The
data files (data.noun, data.verb, data.adj, data.adv) were obtained from
the NLTK wordnet corpus mirror (github.com/nltk/nltk_data,
packages/corpora/wordnet.zip), which packages the unmodified Princeton
WordNet 3.0 database alongside the WordNet licence text.
